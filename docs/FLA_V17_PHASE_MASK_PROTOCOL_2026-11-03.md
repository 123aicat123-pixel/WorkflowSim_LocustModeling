Status: experimental opt-in, original v12/v14 settings unchanged. Earlier read-only DO/EO/SSO analyses suggested, but did not causally show, that local search might be more useful early. Do not promote an algorithm variant before independent results.

For 100 generations, original FLA TF gives exactly 74 DO generations (1–74), 14 EO (75–88), 12 SSO (89–100). DO-only v17 runs 14 local neighbor evaluations per generation in 1–74, and all 30 normal FLA offspring evaluations in 75–100. Exactly 74×14=1036 local probes, 1964 normal FLA operator/bandit opportunities, plus 30 original initialization evaluations = 3030. **Matched late-74** v17 uses local probes in generations 27–100, giving the SAME 1036 local evaluations, 1964 normal and 3030 total. The two windows overlap 27–74; timing differs in 1–26 versus 75–100. Their planned objective evaluation budget and local-probe count match, but offspring histories/RNG paths can diverge. Full v14 uses 1400 local probes and 1600 normal FLA operators. v12 uses 3000 normal FLA operators, zero local probes. None of these methods gets extra evaluations.

Eight predeclared algorithm arms: v12, full v14 random, DO-only v17 random, late-matched v17 random, full v14 timeline, DO-only v17 timeline, late-matched v17 timeline, identical cost-seeded NSGA-II. Every one has same first two seeded low-cost assignments, 3030 stage-in-aware planned fitness calls, and 16 real WorkflowSim Pareto-cover replays of distinct candidate mappings. Population 30, generations 100. Passive selected breeding population diagnostics enabled for the seven FLA variants, already proven ON/OFF neutral.
