## Interpretation gates
- If neither extra8 cohort meaningfully adds real HV, the observed dominated-sector sampling offers little evidence for immediate further K16 rescue-operator tuning.
- If near8 substantially outperforms uniform8 across multiple blocks, this motivates (but does not validate) a separate prospectively tested budget-fair replay selector with new held-out workflow size/family conditions and ≥20 seeds.
- If uniform8 does better or there is strong heterogeneity, reject the cheap-distance proxy as a robust selector and preserve all negative findings.
