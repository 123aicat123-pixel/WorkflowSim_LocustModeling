## Why this diagnostic
- v21 showed original StageInAware planned-front proximity was a weak unreliable proxy for actual HV: FLA near8 − uniform8 mean −0.000166620 across eight known DAG/seed blocks.
- Planned strict-dominance misclassification does not necessarily imply useful true Pareto area; even examples with seven false exclusions sometimes added zero HV.
- Prior v19 already tested queue-risk-based relocation with mixed/negative outcomes. This probe does **not** claim that queue risk or DAG encoding is original; its purpose is to test whether a physically motivated *risk-only structural score* is predictive enough to merit a new independent prospective experiment.

## Exact cohort and pre-frozen scoring features
- 4 DAX families Montage_50/Sipht_60/CyberShake_30/Epigenomics_46 × seeds 991/992 × 2 unchanged solvers (FLA-v12, NSGA-II). For each, compare the same original physical true K16 replay with 16 previously selected extra strictly planned-dominated, simulator-verified candidates: 8 `near-planned-front` and 8 `uniform-remainder`. Both cohorts were selected using planned scores before any real label; the combined pool of 16 is *not a representative random sample of all ~3000 visited*.
- Parse actual committed `config/dax/<workflow>.xml` with WorkflowSim's proven ID translation: <job> elements are enumerated in XML order starting at TaskID 1. Parse <child ref> <parent ref> edges. Preserve exact canonical physical TaskID->VMID mapping; fail closed on missing/duplicate tasks, invalid job count, or a source SHA mismatch across original jobs.
- From each Task→VM genotype compute:
  1. **Queue-load pressure** = max over VMs of assigned sum of DAX job runtimes divided by total DAX job runtime (dimensionless, [0,1]). This is only a cheap static VM contention surrogate, not measured WorkflowSim queue delay.
  2. **Weighted DAG-edge cut pressure** = sum over cross-VM parent→child edges of child DAX runtime divided by sum of child runtimes over ALL DAG edges, dimensionless [0,1]. It approximates dependency transfer pressure, not network traffic bytes.
  3. **Primary structural risk** = 0.5×queue-load pressure + 0.5×weighted DAG-edge cut pressure; fixed equal weights, chosen before computing case results, larger = riskier; no fit, no training or true-label access. Ablate queue-only and edge-only ranks as *secondary descriptive* controls, with no post-hoc best-score promotion.
- For each solver/seed/DAG with exactly the 16 archived dominated-candidate genotypes, select the **top eight** by descending pre-frozen risk score, lexicographic canonical mapping tie-break. Compare real marginal normalized Makespan–Cost HV above unchanged true K16 with: original near8, original uniform8, deterministic random8 (uniform over this 16-candidate conditional pool, fixed 32 seed draws independent of simulator truth), and an *infeasible* exact true-label hindsight `C(16,8)=12870` oracle (diagnostic ceiling only). The model cannot use actual labels in selection or adjust weights after viewing outcomes.
- One shared actual 1.5× reference per DAG/seed, across both solvers and 32 true replays per solver, and 3× sensitivity. No per-candidate reoptimized reference. Each K8 policy receives the same exactly **eight additional real simulator labels**, but since the 16 originally were all observed, ranking them retrospectively is NOT prospective equal-budget scheduling performance.
- Outcome metrics: (i) K8 marginal real HV vs original K16, (ii) fixed-risk − near8 and fixed-risk − uniform8 per blocked case and across FLA-only/NSGA separately, (iii) oracle-available K8 room and calibration diagnostic, (iv) Brier-free descriptive Spearman/precision for positive marginal HV, optional but do not treat within-arm candidates as independent trials. Split descriptive outputs by workflow family; all families previously inspected, NOT a blind test.
- If the structural risk score fails to beat the near8 AND uniform8 controls on FLA across families, reject this mechanism; do not tune weights or sign on these outcomes. If positive, freeze new seeds and truly distinct size/family holdouts BEFORE new true simulator runs. No SCI claim at this stage.
