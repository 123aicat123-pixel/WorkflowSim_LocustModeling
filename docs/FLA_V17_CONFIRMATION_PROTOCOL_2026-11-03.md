# Frozen v17 independent-size confirmation (separate from pilot)

**Candidate frozen:** `v17-do-random`, DO 1–74 from original TF rule, 1036 charged local neighbor evaluations +1964 normal FLA offspring per 100-generation 30-population run; stage-in-aware planning, v12 HV-phase bandit and unchanged planned Pareto-cover K16 true simulator replays.

**Comparator arms:** `v12-hvbandit`, `v17-late-random` (equal 1036 local calls in gens 27–100), `NSGA-II[costseed]`. All four 3030 charged target fitness evaluations, 16 true WorkflowSim simulator replay evaluations per arm, same two deterministic low-cost seeded assignments. One common PLANNED-only reference per DAX/speed computed over all four arms and both seeds: 1.5× maximum makespan/cost +1 primary, 3×+1 sensitivity, never adjusted after true outcomes. Report all failures and negative results.

**Different instances and randomly chosen seeds not used in the earlier v17 pilot:** `Epigenomics_46`, `Inspiral_50`, `CyberShake_100`, `Sipht_100`; speeds default/staggered; optimizer seeds 211 and 212. These files already existed in the repository, and at least some workflow families were explored in earlier v13–v16 work: therefore this is an independent **new-size/seed confirmation cohort only**, NOT a truly new workflow-family or publication-grade holdout. Eight family-speed subgroups × two seeds = 16 paired scenarios. No parameter changes after observing outcome.

Primary evidence: real-simulator HV of frozen DO-only random vs equal-count last-74 random, plus v12 and seeded NSGA-II. Record individual wins/losses and mean relative HV within every family/VM group, not just pooled scores; avoid using 16 correlated scenarios as if all were independent workflow-family replicates. The 3× reference is sensitivity, not an independent trial. Also retain min realized makespan, min realized rental cost, and optimizer search time. Do not promote v17 to default without broad independent families and further seeds.
